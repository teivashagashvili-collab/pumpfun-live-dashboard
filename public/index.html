Honest limits

A qualified setup is a rules-based research candidate, not a guarantee. Pump.fun tokens are frequently manipulated, and past patterns can stop working without warning.

Every qualified call is logged with its entry price, and its real 5m/15m/1h outcome is measured automatically — see the track record and score calibration on the Qualified tab.

Deployer history and social signal only ever adjust a score that already passed every other gate, or exclude a token outright for a proven serial-rug pattern — a clean deployer history or no social data never qualifies a token by itself.
