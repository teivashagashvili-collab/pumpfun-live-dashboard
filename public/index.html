THEORIES

What the agent can reason about

Momentum: persistent short-horizon price/flow can attract more participation, but reversals are common.

Reflexivity: rising price can attract attention, which can increase demand and reinforce price—until the feedback breaks.

Liquidity preference: a move in a thin pool can look spectacular while offering poor exit capacity.

Attention/volume: social attention is difficult to measure directly here, so trading activity is used only as an observable proxy.

Survivorship and base rates: a few spectacular winners can hide a much larger population of failures; the scanner therefore measures rejected tokens too.
